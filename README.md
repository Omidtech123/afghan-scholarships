# Rah — Afghan Scholarship Platform

> Helping Afghan students find opportunities, build stronger applications, and take the next step toward their future.

## Why I Built Rah

I built Rah after seeing how many young Afghans have the ambition to continue their education, but often don't know where to start.

For many students, finding a scholarship is not simply a matter of searching online. Information is scattered across different websites, requirements can be difficult to understand, and many students don't know which opportunities fit their background or how to build a competitive application.

I wanted to create something that could make that first step easier.

Rah brings scholarship opportunities, application resources, and guidance into one platform, with a particular focus on making international opportunities easier to discover for Afghan students.

This project is also a personal reminder that technology can be used not only to build software, but to solve problems that people around us actually face.

## What Rah Does

Rah is a scholarship discovery platform that helps students:

- Discover international scholarship opportunities
- Find opportunities based on their profile and preferences
- Explore detailed information about different scholarships
- Learn how to prepare stronger applications
- Understand requirements such as English tests, CVs, motivation letters, and recommendation letters
- Find resources specifically relevant to Afghan students

## Main Features

### Scholarship Finder
Students can provide information about their background, education level, English proficiency, age, and preferred destination to discover potentially relevant opportunities.

### Scholarship Directory
Browse scholarship opportunities from programs and organizations around the world, including:

- Chevening
- DAAD
- DAFI
- Erasmus Mundus
- Fulbright
- Islamic Development Bank
- MEXT
- Open Doors
- SOLA
- Stipendium Hungaricum
- Türkiye Scholarships
- UWC

### Application Guides

Rah includes practical guides covering:

- Scholarships for Afghan students
- Fully funded scholarships
- Scholarships without IELTS
- University applications
- Scholarship CVs
- Motivation letters
- Recommendation letters
- Extracurricular activities
- English language tests

### Student Resources

The platform also provides resources designed to help students understand the application process and make more informed decisions about their educational opportunities.

## How It Works

1. **Explore** — Discover scholarship opportunities.
2. **Find your match** — Use the scholarship finder to explore opportunities based on your profile.
3. **Prepare** — Learn how to strengthen different parts of your application.
4. **Apply** — Follow the official scholarship information and application requirements.
