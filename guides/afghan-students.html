Scholarships for Afghan students

Focus on eligibility, official information, and an organized application process.

Where to search

Look for international scholarships open to all nationalities, government programmes, university funding, recognized organizations, and programmes that explicitly serve Afghan students or refugees.

Understand eligibility

Rules may depend on nationality, current residence, refugee registration, prior education, age, or visa eligibility. Read every condition. If your situation is complex, ask the official programme a short factual question.

Organize core documents

Keep secure copies of your passport or Tazkira, transcripts, diplomas, translations, language scores, CV, and references. Maintain a list of document expiry dates and translations still needed.

Prepare for different pathways

Some options lead to study in the host country, while others are available in a country of asylum or online. Consider safety, travel, living support, and whether the qualification fits your longer-term goals.

Stay safe online

Use official portals and institutional email addresses. Be cautious with promises of guaranteed admission, scholarship, or visa approval in exchange for payment.

Action checklist

Use the Rah finder, verify three opportunities on official sites, and list the requirements that apply to your own situation.
