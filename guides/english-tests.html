How to prepare for IELTS or TOEFL

Improve deliberately, then book only when your practice scores are ready.

Step 1: confirm the test you need

Check every target programme’s current page. Note accepted tests, score requirement, expiry date, and whether an alternative is allowed. Do not assume all universities accept the same test.

Step 2: take a baseline test

Use official practice material under timed conditions. Identify the weakest skill and set a realistic target score and test date.

Step 3: make a weekly plan

Read and listen daily; practise one timed writing task each week; speak aloud or with a partner; and review your errors. Spend more time correcting recurring mistakes than taking new practice tests.

Improve each skill

Reading and listening need speed and evidence-finding. Writing needs planning, clear paragraphs, and accurate grammar. Speaking needs direct answers, reasons, examples, and confidence with timing.

Track progress

Keep an error notebook for vocabulary, grammar, question types, and timing. Take a full practice test every few weeks under realistic conditions.

Before booking

Leave time for a possible retake and for results to reach universities. Keep your passport or accepted identification ready, and confirm the official test location and policies.
