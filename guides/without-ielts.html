Scholarships without IELTS

“Without IELTS” does not always mean “without English proof.”

Know the difference

A programme may accept TOEFL, Duolingo English Test, a medium-of-instruction letter, an interview, or conditional admission instead of IELTS. Each option has its own conditions.

Check the exact programme rule

Read the current department and scholarship pages—not general lists or old videos. Note the required score, expiry date, alternatives, and whether a waiver applies to your degree.

Medium-of-instruction letters

If accepted, the letter should normally come from your previous institution and state your degree, dates, and language of instruction. It is not automatically accepted; ask admissions first.

Request a waiver correctly

Write a short email with your intended programme, previous degree, years taught in English, and a direct question about accepted proof. Save their written reply.

Use alternatives carefully

Confirm that the scholarship and university both accept the alternative. An admission waiver might not apply later to a visa or a different funding programme.

Keep a backup plan

Prepare for an accepted English test while waiting. A valid score often widens options and reduces uncertainty.
